# DESIGN.md

## Mood & Imagery
- Late-night cinema atmosphere: neon lights reflecting on black marble floors, purple and magenta glows in the dark
- Streaming platform feel: dark immersive experience similar to Netflix/Disney+

## Visual Strategy
- Dark background as base, gradients for depth
- Cards use semi-transparent glassmorphism effect
- Content areas use horizontal scrolling
- Tag/badge system to distinguish content types (HIT/4K/LIVE/HD/NEW)

## Color Palette
- Background: deep black #0a0a0f → #12121a gradient
- Primary accent: magenta/purple #a855f7 → #ec4899 gradient
- Secondary accent: electric blue #3b82f6
- LIVE tag: bright red #ef4444
- Card background: rgba(255,255,255,0.05) semi-transparent
- Text: white #ffffff, secondary text #a1a1aa

## Typography
- Headings: bold, large, with gradient text effects
- Body: regular weight, appropriate line-height
- Tags: small uppercase, bold, rounded background

## Animation & Interaction
- Card hover: slight lift + border glow
- Horizontal scroll areas: smooth scrolling, hidden scrollbar
- Buttons: gradient background + hover glow effect

## Design Don'ts
- No light backgrounds
- No overly complex decorations
- No excessive border-radius on cards (keep restrained)
